# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Founders and operators who need a marketing site, product UI, or native app actually shipped — not a brand deck. Inferred from the live Pixelpoint Studio offer and internal brief; not interviewed this session.

## Product Purpose

Pixelpoint Studio is a service studio (not SaaS). It designs and engineers websites, UI/UX, web apps, and iOS/Android/cross-platform apps, then stays for post-launch SEO and CRO. Design and engineering sit in one room. A dated plan exists before work starts. Contact is name, email, phone, and message; reply within one business day; NDA available. Public contact: `hello@pixelpointstudio.com`. No public pricing.

## Positioning

“Websites and apps built to ship.” Screens that ship, not a brand presentation. Hierarchy first. Webflow for editable marketing sites, React when the product has real logic.

## Operating Context

Live marketing site: `https://pixelpointstudio.com/` (Hostinger static HTML, Bootstrap + Font Awesome). This repository currently holds skills, the identity system, and brand assets — not a rebuild of the live site unless requested.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Offer: websites, UI/UX, web apps, iOS/Android/cross-platform, SEO/CRO after launch.
- Confirmed usable: live site, offer, photography on the live site, email, Next.js-on-Hostinger as a possible stack.
- **Do not invent:** portfolio percent lifts, testimonials, client names, pricing.
- Treat leftover `kreostudio.com` / “Kreo Studio blog style” copy as unverified residue, not product truth.
- Internal names (Rezan, Rana, Omi) are not a public About claim unless the live About page already states them.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: **Pixelpoint** / **Pixelpoint Studio**.
- Keep the lime DNA; do not abandon it for purple, blue, or generic teal. Evolve it into a scarce signal color.
- Identity bar named for this work: Nike wordmark craft, On Running certainty, Linear lettering quality. **Do not copy those marks**, Metalab, or Locomotive.
- The live site’s Arial + `#bad530` + pill-glow system is the anti-reference, not the authority.
- Wordmark and color system were requested to stand in the sports-performance category at billion-dollar craft, for a studio, not a sports product company.

## Evidence on Hand

- Live site HTML/CSS (Arial/Helvetica/system-ui/Roboto, `--accent-green: #bad530`, competing Tailwind blues, 24px pill radius, lime glow blobs, navbar-brand as plain 24px/700 text).
- This repo: `brand/` production SVGs and `DESIGN.md`.
- Absent and must not be fabricated: case-study metrics, testimonials, press.

## Product Principles

1. Ship screens, not theater.
2. One room: design and engineering.
3. Plan dated before work.
4. Proof over claims — if it is not on the live site or in this file, do not write it as fact.
5. The identity should feel inevitable at a glance: one mark idea, one voltage color, no template chrome.
