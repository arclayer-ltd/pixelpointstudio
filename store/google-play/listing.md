## Full description (4000)

```
OwnerShot Ads is for independent shop owners and makers who already have a physical product and a phone.

Photograph a product you own. The app writes original listing copy from that photo and the facts you type: title, price, material, and your own brand name. It makes original stills in square, 4:5, and 9:16, then a 12 to 15 second clip. The export pack holds the square still, the 9:16 still, the spoken script, and Google Play listing text.

You warrant that you own the photos and may advertise the product. The copy model refuses film, celebrity, and third-party brand prompts. Generated audio or audio you recorded is allowed. Licensed music is not. There is no marketplace inventory of goods you do not own, and no picker for other companies’ marks.

How a job runs
1. Shoot or upload a cover photo. Extra shots stay extras.
2. Enter facts. Save stays off until a title and one photo exist.
3. Generate stills or a clip with one Point control. The owner photo is the input.
4. Keep, reject, or regenerate on the still.
5. Watch the 12-second preview. Choose original audio or none.
6. Export when the pack file exists, then share.

Stack named in the product lock: Grok for copy, Higgsfield for stills and motion, Supabase for the account, Vercel for the web app, a dedicated server for the job queue, Google Play for Android.

OwnerShot Ads is published by Pixelpoint Studio.
```
